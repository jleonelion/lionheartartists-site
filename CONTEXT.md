# LionHeart Artists — Talent Intake

LionHeart Artists is a boutique talent management firm representing young performers.
This context covers how prospective talent reaches the firm and how the firm tracks
them from first contact through a representation decision.

## Language

### People

**Parent**:
The legal guardian of a Child. The only person who ever submits an Application —
Children never submit on their own behalf.
_Avoid_: Submitter, applicant, user, customer

**Child**:
A minor (ages 4–18) described in an Application by their Parent. Names the family
relationship, and is the right word when addressing a Parent about their kid.
_Avoid_: Kid, minor, talent, client

**Applicant**:
A Child under consideration for representation. Names the role in the Pipeline
rather than the family relationship: the same person is a Child to their Parent
and an Applicant to the firm.
_Avoid_: Candidate, prospect, lead

**Artist**:
A Child the firm has actually signed to representation. An Applicant becomes an
Artist only on reaching `Signed` — never use it for someone still under review.
_Avoid_: Client, talent, signee

### The process

**Application**:
A complete submission by a Parent asking the firm to consider their Child for
representation. Carries the Child's details and photographs, and is a request for
a decision — not a casual expression of interest.
_Avoid_: Inquiry, interest form, enquiry, request, lead

**Pipeline**:
The record of every Application the firm has received and where each one stands.
_Avoid_: Funnel, CRM, queue, tracker

**Status**:
Where a single Application currently sits in the Pipeline, from `New` through to
`Signed`, `Declined`, or `Archived`. Advanced deliberately by a person, never
inferred.
_Avoid_: Stage, state, phase

**Roster**:
The set of Artists the firm currently represents. What an Applicant is assessed
against for fit.
_Avoid_: Book, client list, portfolio
